## The Core Idea — Property Lookup Chain
When you access a property on an object, JavaScript follows this rule:
Step 1: Look in the object itself
           ↓  (not found?)
Step 2: Look in its prototype
           ↓  (not found?)
Step 3: Look in prototype's prototype
           ↓  (not found?)
Step 4: Reach null → return undefined
In JavaScript, everything is an object and inheritance happens through this prototype chain linkage, making it purely prototypal by nature.
